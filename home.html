Share your pattern

  1. In EaveLuxOS, open your pattern in Create and note its colors, bulb counts, animation, speed and brightness.
  2. Add a small .json file for it to the patterns folder of the library on GitHub. The library's README shows the exact format.
  3. Open a pull request. An automatic check runs on it, and once it's reviewed and merged, your pattern appears here and in every copy of EaveLuxOS.
